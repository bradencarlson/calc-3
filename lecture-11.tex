\content{
}{% presentation
\vspace{3in}
}{% nopresentation
}{% comments
Demonstrate why langrange multipliers work, we want to find the where the level
curves of $f$ just barely touch the curve $g$, that is, where they have the same
tangent line. So either the maximum happens at a critical point of $f$, or where
the tangent lines are a scalar multiple of each other. 
}

\begin{example} Find the extreme values of the function $f(x,y) = x^2+2y^2$
subject to $x^2+y^2=1$.
\end{example}
